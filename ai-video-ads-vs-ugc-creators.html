When a UGC creator is the better pick.

  • You need a real person’s face and voice giving a testimonial.
  • Your product has to be held, tasted or worn on camera to make sense.
  • You want the creator to post it to their own audience too.

When AI ads win.

  • You sell an app or software, so the screen is the product.
  • You want to test lots of hooks fast and keep the ones that work.
  • Your budget is small and every dollar has to go to ad spend.
  • There’s no physical product to ship anywhere.
