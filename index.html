Questions.

How do I pay?

Email us your order and we reply with a secure card payment link. Work starts once it’s paid, and the 48 hours start then.

Can I see the idea before I pay?

Yes. Ask for a free script and we’ll send the 3 hooks we’d use for your product, no charge.

What do you need from me?

Your product or app page link. Photos, a logo or a line about who buys from you help, but they’re optional.

Are the ads made with AI?

Yes, with AI video tools. Each script is written for your product and each ad is checked before it’s sent. An AI person in an ad is never presented as a real customer, and those ads should be posted with the platform’s AI label on.

What if I don’t like them?

Tell us what’s off and we’ll redo them. If the second round still misses, you get your money back.

Who owns the ads?

You do. Run them on any platform, organic or paid.
